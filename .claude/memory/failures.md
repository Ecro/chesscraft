## [fail:design] empty-collection-is-not-absent | 2026-08-05 | count:1

## [fail:design] related-ops-fail-independently | 2026-08-06 | count:1

## [fail:render] unbounded-parent-defeats-inner-scroll | 2026-08-07 | count:1
